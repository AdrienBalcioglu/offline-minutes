Offline Minutes

Record a meeting, get the transcript on your own computer, and write the minutes next to it. The audio is never uploaded.

Transcript

The speech model does not tell speakers apart and makes mistakes on names and figures. Check them before you share the minutes. Ask the people in the room before you record.
